Frequently Asked Questions

What types of houses are commonly available?

Single and double-storey houses, portions, small family homes, apartments and both older and newer construction appear across Pakistani cities.

Should overseas buyers only look in major cities?

Major cities offer services and infrastructure, but value and suitability can also exist in established neighbourhoods and areas near major centres. Match location to purpose.

How do I compare different house types fairly?

Use a consistent checklist: size, condition, utilities, location services, maintenance needs and total cost including any required work.

Is a new house always better than an older one?

Not necessarily. Newer construction can have advantages, but a well-maintained older house in a convenient location can serve a family better than a new house that is poorly situated or over-budget.
